Privacy Policy

Denon Controller does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app communicates only with AV receivers on your local network in order to control the receiver, such as volume, power, and input selection.

No analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking technologies, or third-party data collection tools are used.

Local network access is used solely to connect to and control compatible AV receivers. No local network information is sent to the developer or to any external server.
